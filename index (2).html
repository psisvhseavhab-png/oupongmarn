Intimate 5-Room Eco-Farm & Authentic Khmer Hospitality

Designed for travelers seeking peace away from crowded tourist hubs, Ou Pongmarn Eco-farm offers just 5 thoughtfully crafted rooms surrounded by fruit orchards, native bamboo, and forested hills in Chi Phat.

Whether you are hiking hidden jungle waterfalls, kayaking along the Prek Piphot river at sunrise, or enjoying home-cooked meals picked straight from our organic garden, every moment here moves at nature’s gentle pace.
